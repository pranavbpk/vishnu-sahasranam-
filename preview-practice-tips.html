👂 Listen for
  • Long “aa”: Raa-mo, Vi-raa-mo, Maar-go
  • Shresh-tho: “sh” and “th” together, no “a” between
  • Soft “h” at the end: Na-yah, …ut-ta-mah
Record once, then hear yourself
  • Only after a new verse: say it once on your own.
  • One button: Hear me, then the guide. That is the whole check.
  • No ticks, no sending. “Record again” or “Next” and you are done.
