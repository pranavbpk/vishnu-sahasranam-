🙏 How to do parayanam

  1. Sit comfortably facing the Lord or a lamp. Take a breath and settle.
  2. The chant goes in order: Pūrvāṅgam (opening prayers, 4 parts) → Sahasranāma (108 verses) → Uttarāṅgam (blessings and ending).
  3. Chant with the voice: read the big green lines; the yellow word is being said now.
  4. Lost your place? Look for the yellow word, or tap ◀︎ ▶︎.
  5. Short of time? Chant one part only (tap the part name at the top).
  6. In a group call: keep your microphone off and chant along softly with the leader.
Reading the green lines
Dashes split each word into easy sounds. Long sounds are written double (aa, ee, oo). Pause a little at | and a bit longer at ||. 💡 on each verse gives its own tip.
