How to read the easy sounds
  • Each dash is a beat. Say the pieces slowly, then speed up.
  • aa, ee, oo are held about twice as long as a, i, u.
  • sh sounds like "ship". v is between English v and w.
  • bh, dh are one sound: b or d with a small puff of air.
  • A final h (Vishnuh) is a soft echo of the vowel, like "Vishnu-hu".
  • If your guru or family tradition says it differently, follow them.
