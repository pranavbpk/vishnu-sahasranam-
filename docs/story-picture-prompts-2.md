# Story pictures, batch 2 (8 stories)

Same style as batch 1: bright, friendly, cartoon-and-painting style for children, Indian setting, no text or letters in the picture, landscape 16:9. Lord Vishnu and his forms always with a calm, kind face and blue skin; Krishna as a cheerful child with a peacock feather.

Save each as `img/story/<name>.jpg` (960×540) and `img/story/<name>-s.jpg` (small thumbnail, about 320×180).

1. **narasimha**: Inside a royal palace hall at sunset, a broken stone pillar; Lord Narasimha (lion face, golden mane, four arms, calm and protective, not scary) sits on the doorstep with little Prahlada (about 6, folded hands, smiling) on His lap. Warm orange evening light.
2. **madhusudana**: Lord Vishnu resting on the coiled serpent Adishesha on a calm cosmic ocean; a lotus rises from Him with four-faced Brahma sitting on it, holding glowing palm-leaf books (the Vedas) safely. Two small defeated demons in the background, cartoonish, not frightening. Deep blue and gold.
3. **govinda**: Child Krishna playing the flute in a green meadow near Vrindavan, surrounded by happy cows and calves; a gentle white wish-cow (Surabhi) pours milk over Him; Indra on a white elephant above the clouds, bowing. Morning light.
4. **damodara**: Courtyard of a village house; baby Krishna with butter on His face, tied by a rope around His waist to a wooden grinding mortar; mother Yashoda kneeling, holding the rope ends, smiling and tired; broken butter pot and a few monkeys nearby.
5. **dhruva**: A small boy prince (about 5) sitting cross-legged in meditation in a quiet forest at night, hands folded; Lord Vishnu appears in soft light before him, touching his cheek with a conch; one bright star shining above.
6. **shabari**: An old forest woman with white hair, in a simple sari, offering a leaf-basket of red berries to Lord Rama (bow on shoulder) and Lakshmana outside a small hut; Rama smiling and eating a berry. Green forest, golden afternoon light.
7. **varaha**: Lord Varaha (a huge, noble boar form with a calm face and crown) rising out of a deep blue ocean, holding the round Earth (with tiny mountains, trees and rivers) gently on His tusks; Bhudevi (Mother Earth) as a smiling goddess sitting on it.
8. **gajendra**: A large elephant standing in a lotus lake, trunk raised to the sky holding a pink lotus; Lord Vishnu flying down on Garuda (eagle-man) with the glowing Sudarshana chakra spinning towards a crocodile in the water. Clear sky.
