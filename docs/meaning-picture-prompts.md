# Meaning pictures, verses 61–70

One picture per verse, showing the plain meaning in one simple scene. Same style as the story pictures: bright, friendly, cartoon-and-painting style for children, Indian setting, no text or letters, landscape 16:9. Lord Vishnu always calm and kind with blue skin, four arms only when stated (check the arm count before saving). Keep each scene simple: one main idea, few people.

Save each as `img/mean/vNNN.jpg` (960×540) and `img/mean/vNNN-s.jpg` (small, about 320×180), e.g. `img/mean/v061.jpg`.

1. **v061**: Lord Vishnu (two arms) standing on a hilltop at sunrise, holding a fine bow, a sharp axe resting at His side; with a kind smile He blesses a poor village family below, gold light falling on them. In the corner, sage Vyasa (long white beard) sits under a tree writing on palm-leaf books.
2. **v062**: Lord Vishnu as a gentle healer, holding a golden pot of medicine, placing His hand on the head of a tired, sad old man who is becoming calm and smiling. Behind, three priests chanting with folded hands. Soft dawn light, peaceful river.
3. **v063**: Beautiful child Krishna with a peacock feather in a green meadow, looking kindly at happy cows, calves, deer and birds gathered around Him. A small village family in the distance doing puja at a tulasi plant. Morning light.
4. **v064**: Lord Vishnu (four arms: conch, chakra, mace, lotus) with Goddess Lakshmi shown as a small golden glow on His chest. Two people before Him: on one side a farmer happily holding a full harvest basket, on the other a sage sitting quietly in meditation. Vishnu blesses both. Gold and blue.
5. **v065**: Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi sitting together on a large pink lotus at night, a full moon with soft moonlight behind them. Below, three layers: sky with clouds, the earth with fields and houses, and the ocean, all under His protecting raised hand.
6. **v066**: Smiling Lord Vishnu with beautiful lotus eyes, full of joy, bending down to accept a single flower from a small child; shining heavenly beings in the clouds above bowing. A small oil lamp near the child lights up the scene. Warm evening light.
7. **v067**: Inside a village temple: Lord Vishnu as a temple deity (Ranganatha, reclining on Adishesha), families of all ages standing for darshan; a little girl who was crying now smiles as she looks at Him. Flowers and lamps.
8. **v068**: Lord Vishnu as Trivikrama, huge and glowing, one foot lifted across the sky over the round earth (three steps). Below, small devotees offering lamp aarti at a temple, looking up in wonder. Clear blue sky.
9. **v069**: Lord Vishnu riding Garuda (eagle-man) across the sky, blessing the three worlds below (heaven clouds, earth with villages, the ocean); a small cartoon demon (not scary) running away in the corner. Bright morning.
10. **v070**: A family praying with a lamp in front of a small shrine at home; Lord Vishnu appears in soft light and gently raises His hand in blessing. Glowing palm-leaf scripture books beside Him; a small heap of gold coins looks tiny next to His light. Starry sky outside the window.
